About the Site

The site is designed to mimic a traditional tri-fold board. Though it is optimized for viewing across devices and browsers, I recommend viewing this on a computer so that you can take in all the little details I added (eg, some elements play slight animations on hover). The animations aren't overdone, as I also want to ensure accessibility. This website can be viewed via screen reader.

The Problem

The problem I am trying to solve is honey dripping down the outside of its jar and getting stuck to where the jar is placed.
Before the Honey Catcher :(

The Solution

My solution is The Honey Catcher, which is a food grade and BPA free silicone collar that is placed around the neck of a honey jar to prevent the honey from dripping down the outside of the jar.
After the Honey Catcher!

Sales

Sales will be done in physical stores via word-of mouth marketing.
